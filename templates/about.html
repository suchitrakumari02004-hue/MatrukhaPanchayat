Who We Are

Welcome to Gram Panchayat Matrukha

Gram Panchayat Matrukha is committed to promoting village development, transparency, public awareness and accessible citizen services.

Our digital information portal is designed to make Panchayat-related information and services more accessible, convenient and transparent.

A Connected Village

Connecting citizens with information, digital services and opportunities for community development.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a digitally empowered, clean, developed and self-reliant village.

Our Mission

To promote transparency, citizen participation, digital awareness and convenient access to public services.

Our Core Objectives

Promote transparency and accountability in local administration.

Make important Panchayat information easily accessible to citizens.

Encourage active citizen participation in village development.

Spread awareness about government schemes and public welfare services.

Provide a convenient platform for citizens to submit complaints and suggestions.

Promote digital awareness and community development.
